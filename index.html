About Me

I’m a U.S. Navy veteran and cybersecurity student at Robert Morris University with an interest in the intersection of technical security, governance, risk, and policy. My background in the Navy’s Nuclear Reactor Department shaped how I think about accountability, documentation, and operating in mission-critical environments where trust has to be supported by evidence.

My work now focuses on security operations, AI security, research, governance, risk, and compliance. Through my Brixmor Property Group internship and AI security research, I am especially interested in how organizations evaluate controls, manage risk, and document evidence for security requirements.
