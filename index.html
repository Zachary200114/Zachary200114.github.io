About Me

I’m a U.S. Navy veteran and cybersecurity student at Robert Morris University. Before starting college, I spent time in the Navy’s Nuclear Reactor Department, where I learned to work carefully, troubleshoot under pressure, and take documentation and accountability seriously. Those habits have carried directly into the way I approach cybersecurity.

During my internship with Brixmor Property Group, I gained hands-on experience with security tickets, vulnerability management, domain assessments, and third-party risk. Outside of work, I enjoy building security projects and researching how AI is changing areas like phishing, security controls, and risk.

What interests me most is understanding how systems work, finding where they can fail, and helping organizations make better security decisions. I’m continuing to develop both the technical and risk-focused sides of my experience as I prepare for a career in cybersecurity.
